# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – MS Excel Dashboard

This project showcases an **interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel**, designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends across multiple store locations.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into **clear, actionable business insights** using Excel’s analytical and visualization features, making it suitable for **data analyst and business analyst portfolios**.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:
- Analyze coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Identify sales patterns by time, location, product category, and customer behavior
- Present insights through a single interactive dashboard

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

- **Total Sales:** $698,812.33  
- **Total Footfall:** 149,116  
- **Average Bill per Person:** 4.69  
- **Average Orders per Person:** 1.44  

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall business performance.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 🔹 Quantity Ordered by Hour
- Displays hourly order volume
- Identifies peak ordering times
- Supports staffing and inventory planning

---

### 🔹 Category-wise Sales Distribution
- Shows percentage contribution of each product category
- Coffee contributes the highest share of total sales
- Helps optimize product strategy

---

### 🔹 Order Size Distribution
- Breakdown of Small, Regular, and Large orders
- Highlights customer purchase preferences

---

### 🔹 Store Location Performance
- Compares sales and footfall across:
  - Astoria
  - Hell’s Kitchen
  - Lower Manhattan
- Identifies high-performing and low-performing locations

---

### 🔹 Top 5 Products by Sales
- Highlights best-selling products such as:
  - Barista Espresso
  - Brewed Chai Tea
  - Gourmet Brewed Coffee
- Useful for promotions and inventory planning

---

### 🔹 Orders by Weekday
- Analyzes order volume across all days of the week
- Helps identify high-demand days

---
